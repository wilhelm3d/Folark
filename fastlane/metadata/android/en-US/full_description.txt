Folark is a home-screen launcher for foldable phones, built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3. It keeps a separate layout for the cover screen and the inner screen and works as a plain launcher on any phone.

Features:

* Home screen with free icon placement, widgets, widget stacks and folders, and drag and drop between pages, the dock and the drawer.
* App drawer with search for apps, shortcuts, contacts (opt-in), Android settings pages, quick actions and a calculator; tabs, folders, an A–Z bar and a two-pane layout when unfolded.
* A taskbar on the inner screen: pinned and recent apps, media, split-screen controls, pull handles, themes and animations.
* Folark's own Recents: open apps as cards, split view for up to three apps, pop-up windows, pinned cards and, with root, screenshots and memory use.
* Split screen from the taskbar, Recents or home, with a picker beside the first app.
* Fold and unfold actions, transitions and app pairs.
* Notification dots and counts, notification previews in long-press menus, people and media widgets.
* Icon packs, themed icons, Liquid Glass, AMOLED dark and fonts.
* Optional extras with ADB rights, root or Folark's Vector (LSPosed) module.
* Backup and restore of layout and settings.

Free and open-source under the Apache-2.0 license.
